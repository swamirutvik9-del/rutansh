### Why Attention Is Not the Same as Impact

Today, being seen is easy.  
Being impactful is not.

### The Rise of Visibility

People speak more.  
Post more.  
Show more.  
Visibility has increased.  
But visibility is not the same as value.

### The Difference

Attention comes from being noticed.  
Impact comes from creating change.  
One is immediate.  
The other is slow.

### Why Attention Wins

Attention is measurable.  
Likes, views, shares.  
Impact is not always visible.  
It appears over time.  
So systems reward attention more than impact.

### The Long-Term Problem

When attention becomes the goal,  
depth reduces.  
People focus on being seen —  
not on being useful.

### What Actually Lasts

Attention fades quickly.  
Impact stays.  
Not because it is loud —  
but because it is built carefully.

What feels normal today shapes what becomes unavoidable tomorrow.
